## 38. Seasonal content
A season adds a small genetic expansion and never invalidates old populations.

Example:
```text
Season: ABYSS
Bioluminescence
Pressure resistance
Deep pigment
Tentacle morphology
Void pattern
```

After the season, alleles remain in world population through descendants.
